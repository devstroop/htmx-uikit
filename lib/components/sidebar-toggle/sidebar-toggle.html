SidebarToggle

Hamburger toggle for collapsible sidebars (react SidebarToggle parity): a 40px .dx-sidebar-toggle target, usually placed in the header. It carries its own accessible name (aria-label, never icon-only without one) and flips the sidebar — pair with data-dx-sidebar-toggle in app markup, or wire aria-expanded as below. Presentational only.
