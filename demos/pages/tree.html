Keyboard

KeyAction
ArrowDown/ArrowUpMove focus between visible nodes (wraps around)
ArrowRightExpand the focused node, then move to its first child
ArrowLeftCollapse the focused node, or move to its parent
Home/EndJump to the first / last visible node
EnterSelect the focused node
SpaceSelect — or toggle the focused checkbox when checkboxes are on
a–z/0–9Type-ahead: jump to the next node matching the typed prefix
