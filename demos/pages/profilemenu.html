ProfileMenu

Avatar trigger with account actions.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter/Space/ArrowDownOpen the menu from the trigger with the first item active
ArrowDown/ArrowUpMove the active item, wrapping and skipping disabled
Home/EndJump to the first / last enabled item
Enter/SpaceActivate the active item, close and refocus the trigger
EscapeClose the menu and return focus to the trigger
TabClose the menu
