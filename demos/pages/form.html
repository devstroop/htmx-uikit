Form

One validated form layout wiring labels, hints, and errors together.

Submit validates rules (data-dx-required, data-dx-email, data-dx-minlength): failures block the submit, render into [data-dx-field-error], set aria-invalid and dispatch dx:invalid; editing clears the error; a valid submit dispatches dx:submit with FormData.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMove to the next field or the submit button
Shift+TabMove back to the previous control
EnterSubmit the form from a text field (implicit submission)
